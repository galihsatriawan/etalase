## Headline numbers (deck slide 2)

### 1. US$185B SEA e-commerce GMV in 2025; consumer interest in AI 3× the global average

- **Claim as stated:** "US$185B Southeast Asia e-commerce GMV in 2025; consumer interest in AI is 3× the global average."
- **What the source says:**
  - "E-commerce is picking up, with GMV and revenue projected to reach $185 billion and $41 billion respectively in 2025."
  - "…with consumer interest in AI topics 3x higher than the global average."
  - Also: "Video commerce now accounts for approximately 25% of total GMV."
- **Source:** e-Conomy SEA 2025 (Google, Temasek, Bain), press release, Singapore, 11 Nov 2025.
  Primary: [Bain press release](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/sea/e-conomy-sea-2025/).
  Secondary: [Temasek news release](https://www.temasek.com.sg/en/news-and-resources/news-room/news/2025/e-conomy-sea-2025-report-aseans-digital-economy-poised-to-surpass-300-billion) (linked in docs; not re-opened today).
- **Status:** ✅ verified
- **Nuance:** The 2025 figure is a full-year *projection* published in November 2025, not an audited total.
  "Interest in AI" is measured as interest in AI topics (search/attention), not purchases made by AI.
  Say "projected US$185B" if pressed.

### 2. 95% of Indonesian online businesses sell through chat apps; only 18% use marketplaces (BPS, 2023)

- **Claim as stated:** "95% of Indonesian online businesses sell through chat apps such as WhatsApp; only 18% use marketplaces (BPS, 2023)."
- **What the source says:**
  - Databoks: "The majority, or 95.33%, of Indonesian e-commerce businesses sell through instant messaging applications" … "33.29% sell on social media" … "only 17.80% utilize marketplaces" … "1.77% through websites".
  - GoodStats (Indonesian): "95,33% usaha" use instant messaging; "33,29% usaha e-commerce juga tercatat memanfaatkan media sosial".
- **Source:**
  - Primary: BPS-Statistics Indonesia, *E-Commerce Statistics 2023* (Statistik E-Commerce 2023), catalog no. 8101004, released 30 Jan 2025: [BPS publication page](https://www.bps.go.id/en/publication/2025/01/30/d52af11843aee401403ecfa6/statistik-e-commerce-2023.html). The page is reachable; its abstract does **not** show the channel table, and the PDF download could not be opened from our environment (see failed URLs), so we could not read the exact table number. Open the PDF from that page before the pitch and note the table.
  - Secondary: [Databoks (Katadata), 7 Feb 2025](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/technology-telecommunications/statistics/67a5866310206/few-local-e-commerce-players-sell-on-marketplaces); [GoodStats, 28 Feb 2025](https://goodstats.id/article/mayoritas-usaha-e-commerce-berjualan-lewat-aplikasi-pesan-instan-HmFph).
- **Status:** ⚠️ verified with nuance (numbers confirmed by two secondary sources quoting BPS; BPS table not read directly)
- **Nuance:** BPS counts *businesses that sold or took orders online* in 2023, and one business can use several channels
  (the shares add up to well over 100%; 151.5% in total). So it is "95% use chat", not "95% sell only via chat". Survey sample per Databoks:
  40,011 businesses across 38 provinces. The base is "businesses that used the internet to take orders or sell" in 2023, so a food stall that takes orders on WhatsApp is counted.
  The figure is a floor effect of that definition: it shows that chat is the default order inbox, **not** that 95% of sellers have no store system. BPS does not publish the overlap between chat and marketplace sellers,
  and no source measures product sellers who are chat-first. The 2024 edition (*Statistik E-Commerce 2024*, published 28 Nov 2025, about 4.4 million businesses per GoodStats; Databoks gives about 3.81 million "in 2024", which looks like the 2023 total) exists and its channel table has not been read.
  Suggested sentence: "BPS (2023, 40,011 businesses, multiple answers): 95.33% of e-commerce businesses use instant messaging to sell, 33.29% social media, 17.80% marketplaces, 1.77% a website. Chat is the default order inbox; the share of chat-only sellers is not measured."

### 3. ~1 in 2 Southeast Asian shoppers have bought or sold on social media or messaging apps (2019)

- **Claim as stated:** "~1 in 2 Southeast Asian shoppers have bought or sold on social media or messaging apps (2019 survey)."
- **What the source says:**
  - KrASIA: "Close to half of the shoppers said they have bought or sold on social media or messaging platforms." Thailand "over 40% of the respondents", "Vietnam with 36%", "United States (5%)".
  - e27: "Approximately 47 per cent said they conduct conversational commerce on social media or messaging platforms." Lists "Indonesia (29 per cent)". Study conducted May–August 2019.
- **Source:** Facebook × Boston Consulting Group conversational-commerce study (2019). The original report was not found online;
  press coverage: [KrASIA, 1 Nov 2019](https://kr-asia.com/shoppers-in-southeast-asia-prefer-to-buy-using-chat-or-voice-applications-according-to-joint-study);
  [e27, Oct 2019](https://e27.co/southeast-asia-emerges-as-leader-in-conversational-commerce-thailand-vietnam-most-advanced-in-adoption-20191031/).
  KrASIA: "There were 5,391 respondents from Southeast Asia."
- **Status:** ⚠️ verified with nuance; **do not use as a headline for Indonesia**
- **Nuance:** This is 2019 data, funded by Facebook, and the most recent *regional, cross-country* comparison we found. The two articles are not identical: KrASIA writes "close to half" (5,391 respondents) while e27 gives 47% social media or chat,
  44% e-commerce platforms and 9% brand sites (8,864 respondents, nine countries), which suggests a split of channels rather than "47% of all shoppers" (our inference, not stated by either article). The conversation definition also includes chat inside e-commerce apps.
  The Indonesia-specific figure was **29%**, lower than Thailand (40%) or Vietnam (36%), so never say "half of Indonesians". Use BPS 2023 for Indonesia or drop this claim.

### 4. 11% of SMB merchants agent-ready vs 19% of large; 15% have structured product data

- **Claim as stated:** "11% of SMB merchants agent-ready vs 19% of large merchants; 15% have structured product data."
- **What the source says:**
  - "Just 11% of small- to medium-sized businesses (SMBs) qualify as agent-ready, compared with 19% of large merchants."
  - "15% of merchants overall have structured product data for agent access."
- **Source:** PYMNTS Intelligence × Visa Acceptance Solutions, *2026 Global Digital Shopping Index: Merchant Edition*.
  Primary: [PYMNTS, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.pymnts.com/smbs/2026/visa-says-small-merchants-could-move-faster-on-ai-shopping/).
  Secondary: [Forkast, 23 Sep 2026](https://forkast.news/?p=130650) ("only 15% of merchants possess the structured product data necessary for agents").
- **Status:** ✅ verified
- **Nuance:** The 15% is *all merchants*, not SMBs only. It is a global merchant survey, not Indonesia-specific; our
  sellers (no store system at all) are likely below the SMB average.

### 6. AI agents already check out through Google's UCP (US live, Australia starting, India in testing)

- **Claim as stated:** "AI agents already check out through Google's UCP in the US, Australia and India." (too strong, see below)
- **What the sources say:**
  - US: "US shoppers can buy items available on Etsy and Wayfair" in AI Mode or Gemini ([Retail Brew, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.retailbrew.com/stories/2026/02/11/google-makes-etsy-and-wayfair-items-shoppable-within-agentic-ai-search)).
  - Australia: "Australia is the first market in the Asia-Pacific region to go live with the protocol." ([Ragtrader, 19 Jun 2026](https://www.ragtrader.com.au/news/the-iconic-kogan-test-google-s-agentic-ai-checkout)).
  - Google itself, 20 May 2026: UCP-powered checkout "will roll out across Canada and Australia in the coming months, and later to the U.K." ([Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/shopping-updates-google-marketing-live)).
  - PPC Land, 7 Oct 2026: the Merchant Center UCP hub rolls out only in the US and only to merchants Google selects; "Australia and Canada to follow next year". Indonesia is not mentioned ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/canada-and-australia-face-2027-wait-for-googles-merchant-center-ucp-hub/)).
  - India: "The early test is limited to some users and a small selection of products." TechCrunch adds it is unclear what technology powers it ([TechCrunch, 26 Sep 2026](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3170226)).
- **Status:** ⚠️ verified with nuance. The US is live. Australia is a rollout with a few retailers (Ragtrader, Shopifreaks), while Google's own pages still describe the checkout as US-only and the self-serve hub as arriving in Australia in 2027, so say "started in Australia with select retailers", not "live". India is a **limited test** (Flipkart "Buy" in Gemini/AI Mode), and TechCrunch notes it may not use Google-hosted UCP checkout.
- **Nuance:** Say "live in the US, started with select retailers in Australia, testing in India with Flipkart". Not live in Indonesia, and no date is known. The sentence "The protocol will expand to markets including India, Indonesia, and Latin America"
  is **InfoQ's own narration** (19 Jan 2026), not a Google quote ([InfoQ](https://infoq.com/news/2026/01/google-agentic-commerce-ucp)). Google's own post of 11 Jan 2026 names only "eligible U.S. retailers", and Google's May and October 2026 posts do not mention Indonesia.
  Onboarding is by Google's selection and needs a payment provider that can process Google Pay tokens. Earlier versions of this document labelled "Google names Indonesia a UCP market" as verified; the label is now ⚠️ partly: one trade report says so, Google's pages do not.

## Likely judge questions

**1. Why won't Shopee or Meta just do this?**
Shopee is bringing *its own listings* to AI (Shopee app in ChatGPT, discovery then checkout on Shopee; a Google prototype)
([Sea](https://www.sea.com/news/406), [FashionNetwork](https://uk.fashionnetwork.com/news/Google-shopee-owner-sea-to-develop-ai-tools-for-e-commerce,1808880.html)).
That keeps sellers inside marketplace fees (10% for fashion,
[Metro TV News](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/NxGCP3en-shopee-kerek-biaya-admin-mulai-2026-segini-nominalnya)).
Only 17.80% of Indonesian e-commerce businesses use marketplaces at all
([Databoks/BPS](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/technology-telecommunications/statistics/67a5866310206/few-local-e-commerce-players-sell-on-marketplaces)).
Meta now offers a free AI sales agent inside WhatsApp Business (Meta Business Agent, reported by the press as launched for Indonesian businesses on 11 Aug 2026; rollout, pricing and payment support in Indonesia are unconfirmed) that answers buyers and recommends products from the seller's existing catalog.
On the pages we read, it does not build a catalog from photos, run QRIS checkout or open the catalog to third-party agents, but Meta could add them. If it does, what remains is catalog quality, stock reservation and the payout hold.
We are a customer of WhatsApp, not a competitor, and the closest local rivals are Mayar (storefront, QRIS, couriers, a merchant-side MCP server) and cshub (AI orders, Biteship, Midtrans QRIS).

**2. Is agent commerce live in Indonesia?**
No, and we say so. AI discovery is live in Indonesia (Google AI Mode in Indonesian since September 2025; Google AI shopping from March 2026; Shopee in ChatGPT, which hands over to Shopee to pay), but we found no checkout by an agent and no report of an agent-initiated purchase in Indonesia, and OpenAI scaled back its own checkout in March 2026. UCP checkout is live in the US ([Retail Brew](https://www.retailbrew.com/stories/2026/02/11/google-makes-etsy-and-wayfair-items-shoppable-within-agentic-ai-search)),
started with select retailers in Australia ([Ragtrader](https://www.ragtrader.com.au/news/the-iconic-kogan-test-google-s-agentic-ai-checkout)), is in test in India
([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3170226)), and Indonesia was named as a future market in January
([InfoQ](https://infoq.com/news/2026/01/google-agentic-commerce-ucp), a trade report, not a Google statement) with no date since. Google's UCP payment handlers are card-based, not QRIS.
Our MCP server works with an agent that its owner connects to our URL (there is no automatic discovery), and the catalog and QRIS store link are useful without any agent. We treat the agent door as an option, not a revenue plan.

**3. How big is the chat-commerce segment?**
BPS: 95.33% of Indonesian e-commerce businesses use instant messaging to sell (multiple answers; a floor effect of BPS's wide definition of e-commerce business)
([BPS](https://www.bps.go.id/en/publication/2025/01/30/d52af11843aee401403ecfa6/statistik-e-commerce-2023.html),
[Databoks](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/technology-telecommunications/statistics/67a5866310206/few-local-e-commerce-players-sell-on-marketplaces)).
Regionally, SEA e-commerce GMV is a projected US$185B for 2025
([Bain](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/sea/e-conomy-sea-2025/)). We do not claim a GMV for chat commerce
specifically, because no source measures it, nor the share of product sellers who are chat-first. That is the first thing the seller interviews have to size.

**4. Isn't the 2019 data old?**
Yes. It is the only cross-country SEA comparison we found
([KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com/shoppers-in-southeast-asia-prefer-to-buy-using-chat-or-voice-applications-according-to-joint-study)), it was funded by Facebook, and for Indonesia it says 29%.
For Indonesia we use BPS 2023 data published January 2025, which is newer and official (the 2024 edition is out and not yet read).

**5. Why would sellers pay 4%?**
It is below the 10% Shopee fashion admin fee plus Rp1,250 per order
([Metro TV News](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/NxGCP3en-shopee-kerek-biaya-admin-mulai-2026-segini-nominalnya)), but not below Shopee's low-fee categories (from 2.5%) and about the same as Mayar (3% + 0.7% QRIS, no monthly fee, [Mayar](https://mayar.id/pricing)).
It is charged only on delivered orders, with no monthly fee. The honest answer is that willingness to pay 3–5%, and whether sellers prefer a commission to a subscription, is our first assumption to validate (doc 10), and a pure 4% commission only
covers the fixed per-seller costs above roughly 20 orders per seller per month.

**6. What does AI cost you per order?**
About IDR 90 on Gemini 2.5 Flash and about IDR 380 on Gemini 3.8 Flash at 2027 list prices, roughly 6% of a IDR 6,800 commission
([Vertex AI pricing](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/pricing)). The bigger costs are WhatsApp messages
(~IDR 357–460 each and 7–9 charged messages per order since 1 October 2026 in our estimate, [Qontak](https://help-center.qontak.com/hc/en-us/articles/48430892733465-Latest-WhatsApp-Message-Prices-October-2026)),
the payment platform's per-account fee and the gateway's fixed fee ([Xendit](https://xendit.co/en-id/pricing/)), which is why buttons, short combined messages and a message meter matter. Doc 10 has the margin by orders per seller.

**7. Are small merchants really behind?**
Yes: 11% of SMBs are agent-ready vs 19% of large merchants, and only 15% of merchants have structured product data
([PYMNTS × Visa](https://www.pymnts.com/smbs/2026/visa-says-small-merchants-could-move-faster-on-ai-shopping/)).
Chat sellers without a store system are below that.

**8. Isn't Shopify/Stripe already doing this?**
Shopify serves brands with structured catalogs, including non-Shopify brands through its Agentic plan
([Shopify](https://shopify.com/news/agentic-commerce-momentum)). Stripe's suite targets website platforms
([Stripe](https://stripe.com/us/newsroom/news/agentic-commerce-suite)), and Stripe in Indonesia is invite-only and supports only
virtual-account transfers ([Stripe Support](https://support.stripe.com/questions/requirements-to-open-a-stripe-account-in-indonesia)).
Neither builds a catalog from WhatsApp photos or settles via QRIS.

**9. Is the agent market big enough to matter?**
Not yet, and we do not build the plan on it. Morgan Stanley projects US$190–385B of US online spend through agents by 2030
([Morgan Stanley](https://www.morganstanley.com/insights/articles/agentic-commerce-market-impact-outlook)), a US scenario. Alibaba's Qwen
handled 120M+ orders in six days in a subsidised campaign in China
([SCMP](https://scmp.com/tech/article/3343289/alibabas-qwen-tops-120-million-orders-6-days-amid-chinas-ai-shopping-battle)),
which is not evidence for Indonesia. Closer to the question, OpenAI scaled back its checkout in March 2026 and Gartner found willingness to hand purchase decisions to AI "topped out at 11%" in the US
([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-27-gartner-survey-finds-consumers-want-ai-shopping-help-but-not-ai-purchase-decisions)).
People accept AI for searching and comparing; the store link serves them today and the agent door is an option.

**10. What about tax and data protection?**
Marketplace PPh 22 collection (PMK 37/2025) was postponed to 31 Oct 2026
([IKPI](https://ikpi.or.id/en/djp-tunda-pungutan-pph-pasal-22-marketplace-hingga-31-oktober-2026/)); we would apply any withholding
at payout through the country pack. UU 27/2022 (PDP) has applied fully since 17 Oct 2024
([BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798), [Detik](https://inet.detik.com/law-and-policy/d-7597590/uu-pdp-sudah-berlaku-ini-dampak-pelanggar-kebocoran-data));
we keep KYC and bank data at the payment provider. Two items need counsel: PP 33/2026 (the PDP implementing regulation, in force mid-January 2027, with a data protection officer and fines up to 2% of annual revenue) and whether holding payouts makes Etalase a payment-service activity (see doc 09).

**11. Does Xendit really let you hold the seller's money?**
We do not know yet, and we do not claim it. No Xendit page we read documents a platform-controlled hold: sub-account users withdraw from their own dashboard, and in Indonesia auto-withdrawal can be changed but not paused. We built the hold behind a provider interface and are asking Xendit in writing (and looking at DOKU's custom hold periods) before any real money moves.

**12. Who else does catalog-from-photos?**
We did not find a competitor page that claims it (Meta reads an existing catalog; Mayar has a landing-page generator). That rests on marketing pages and a limited search, not on testing their products, and the quality of our extraction has not been measured with Gemini yet.

**13. What about cash on delivery?**
Not offered in the pilot, on purpose ([ADR 0009](adr/0009-qris-only-no-cod.md)): the seller would pack on a promise, and the payout hold and refund flow assume the money arrived first.
COD is common in Indonesia (NielsenIQ 2023 put it at 56% in five cities; one vendor claims over 90%; neither figure is ours), so this may cost buyers. We measure it: every order records its door and the operator report gives the
share of checkouts that expired unpaid. *Nuance:* an expired checkout does not prove the buyer wanted COD; the seller interviews ask directly.

**14. Where is the hold written down, and who decides a dispute?**
`/policy/payouts` (and `.json`): numbers read from the live settings, an Etalase operator decides, the longest wait is computed, no "rekber". The appeal paragraph shows only when a support contact is configured, and nobody is staffed for it yet.

## Market validation, 16 October 2026

A five-way review of the background and goals (Indonesian competitors, agentic-commerce players, seller and buyer reality, agent adoption in Indonesia, platform economics and regulation) produced a claim register of 40 claims:
11 verified as written, 18 needing correction, 6 unsupported or unresolved, 4 partly supported and 1 new risk. The sections above carry the corrected rows. The most important findings, in order of how much they change the plan:

1. **Hold feasibility on Xendit is undocumented** and the legal classification of holding payouts is open (PBI 23/6/PBI/2021 lists disbursement to goods and services providers as a payment-service activity). Both are questions for Xendit and counsel, in writing, before real money.
2. **Per-seller fixed costs** (WhatsApp messages since 1 Oct 2026, a possible Rp25,000 per active account per month, Biteship Rp149,000 per month from 2 Nov 2026) make a pure 4% commission comfortable only above about 20 orders per seller per month. The pilot plan assumes 10.
3. **The competitive space is narrower**: Meta Business Agent and Mayar occupy most of "AI plus WhatsApp plus catalog plus pay". What remains unfilled on the pages we read is catalog-from-photos and the payout hold with returns.
4. **Agent demand in Indonesia is unmeasured.** AI discovery is live, agent checkout is not, and the agent door is an option.
5. **No seller or buyer has been interviewed.** Willingness to pay, to wait 72 to 168 hours, return rates, orders per month and the size of the chat-first segment have no data.
